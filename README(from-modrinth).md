# PolyOpti
[![Fabric](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/intergrav/devins-badges/refs/heads/v3/assets/compact/supported/fabric_46h.png)](https://fabricmc.net/) [![OneConfig](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/akkpwr/akkpwr-resources/refs/heads/main/butbetter/badges/requires_oneconfig_46h.png)](https://modrinth.com/mod/oneconfig) [![Java25](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/intergrav/devins-badges/refs/heads/v3/assets/compact/built-with/java25_46h.png)](https://adoptium.net/temurin/releases/?version=25&os=any&arch=any)

**PolyOpti** is a lightweight, open-source optimization mod built for **OneConfig**. The goal is simple: squeeze out extra frames, cut down render overhead in crowded scenes, and give you direct control over what the game actually draws on screen.

Most performance mods either hide everything behind rigid presets or scatter settings across cluttered menus. PolyOpti keeps every tweak inside a single, clean **OneConfig** interface where you can adjust things on the fly, switch profiles between servers, or reset any option in one click.

> ### Alpha & Experimental Notice
> PolyOpti is currently in **alpha** and **experimental**. Expect rough edges, potential crashes or visual glitches—especially in large modpacks or alongside other render/culling mods. Performance gains depend heavily on your hardware, scene, and mod list: FPS may stay roughly the same, or even drop lower than without the mod depending on your settings and setup. Test carefully and [report issues](https://github.com/akkpwr/PolyOpti/issues) with logs if something breaks.

### What makes it worth running?

- **Built around OneConfig** — All settings live in one clean, searchable menu with instant profile switching. No messy config files or extra screen overlays.
- **VulkanMod-friendly** — Written with modern rendering pipelines in mind. It works smoothly alongside **VulkanMod** and other Fabric performance mods without legacy OpenGL hacks or weird rendering conflicts.
- **Cut the clutter, keep the frames** — Fine-tune entity and block-entity render distances, strip out unnecessary visual noise, and keep frame times steady when things get chaotic.
- **Zero bloat** — Focused on low per-frame overhead and clean mixins. It only touches what actually matters for performance.
